# 12. Inclusion Criteria

The selected studies satisfied the following criteria:

* Published in peer-reviewed journals or conference proceedings.
* Written in English.
* Published between 2020 and 2026.
* Related to RUL prediction, PHM, predictive maintenance, or bearing prognostics.
* Utilized deep learning models such as CNN, LSTM, BiLSTM, CNN-LSTM, or hybrid architectures.
* Investigated optimization algorithms for machine learning or deep learning models.
* Included Firefly Algorithm or related metaheuristic optimization approaches.

---

# 13. Exclusion Criteria

Studies were excluded if they:

* Were unrelated to prognostics or predictive maintenance.
* Focused solely on fault diagnosis without RUL prediction.
* Did not employ machine learning or deep learning methods.
* Were duplicate publications.
* Lacked sufficient methodological details.

# 15. Expected Outcome

The search strategy aims to identify relevant literature that supports:

1. The effectiveness of CNN-LSTM architectures for bearing RUL prediction.
2. The application of deep learning in prognostics and health management.
3. The role of metaheuristic algorithms in automated hyperparameter optimization.
4. The potential of Firefly Algorithm for improving CNN-LSTM performance in Remaining Useful Life prediction.

The collected literature forms the foundation for the literature review, research gap identification, and development of the proposed Firefly-Optimized CNN-LSTM framework.
